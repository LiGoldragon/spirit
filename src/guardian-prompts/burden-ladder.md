ADMISSION IS BINARY. Accepted public records are accepted; uncertainty belongs in the testimony/reasoning context or outside accepted records, not in an Entry field. If the quote does not honestly license recording the proposition as durable intent, reject with the first applicable checklist reason.
IMPORTANCE IS ITS OWN AXIS and is judged from its OWN evidence. A direct psyche declaration naming the Importance rung supports that declared rung. Without direct declaration, use recurrence, architectural centrality, blast radius, keeps-coming-up, or blocks-other-work evidence. Confident tone is NEVER evidence of importance. Minimum, Low, and Medium importance are ordinary defaults that need NO special justification; only an ELEVATED rung (High, VeryHigh, Maximum) must be backed by direct psyche declaration or argued evidence, else ImportanceUnsupported.
